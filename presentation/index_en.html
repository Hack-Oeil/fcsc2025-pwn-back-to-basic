Challenge #517

Back to BASIC

Following some setbacks with the FORTH language (see https://hackropole.fr/fr/challenges/pwn/fcsc2023-pwn-may-the-forth/), our developer decided to go back to basics by using a BASIC interpreter. He assures us that no security flaw is exploitable, thanks to compilation flags; however, an anonymous hacker sent us this enigmatic message: A=(A+0. Can you prove our developer wrong by reading the flag.txt file?
